WHO WE ARE

An NGO Built On
Listening first

Established on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 non‑profit organization dedicated to driving meaningful social change. Headquartered in Chhattisgarh and working across India, the foundation tackles pressing social challenges through a strong network of professionals and volunteers. Credibility & Compliance Holds 80G and 12A certifications, ensuring donor trust through transparency, accountability, and tax‑exempt benefits. Registered under CSR‑1, enabling partnerships with corporations to deliver impactful Corporate Social Responsibility (CSR) initiatives. Guided by its mission of sustainable development and inclusive growth, InAmigos Foundation continues to empower communities and create long‑term positive impact nationwide.
